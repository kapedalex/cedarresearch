Why Cedar exists

Understanding the danger.
Making it tangible.

The capabilities of AI are advancing faster than public understanding of the risks.

Cedar studies threats from current and future AI systems — from mass surveillance and manipulation to the catastrophic consequences of losing control of more capable systems.

Our aim is to turn these risks into concrete evidence people can examine. We start with a technical audience and work to translate the findings for policymakers, the media, and the wider public.

Meet the people behind Cedar

Help technical and policy audiences understand the findings and their implications.
