Research · Privacy & surveillance

AI and mass surveillance

Large-Scale Online Deanonymization With LLMs

AI can connect fragments of public information to identify people behind anonymous online accounts. This research examines how language models could make deanonymization possible at scale.

The question

What happens to privacy when the cost of investigating a person falls dramatically?

The broader research direction includes studying AI systems as personal investigators: what they can find, what they infer, and how people respond to those findings.

Research · AI security

Tiered Disclosure

Strategic Disclosures of LLM Security Weaknesses

Finding a security weakness is only part of the problem. How that finding is reported can shape whether a developer repairs a single example or addresses a broader class of failures.

The question

How can evaluators share actionable evidence while keeping future tests meaningful?

This project studies disclosure at different levels of abstraction: describing the failure class, reporting evidence about its breadth, and preserving private test instances for independent rechecks.

Under the Mask

Под маской

A friendly conversational interface makes an AI system feel familiar. This interactive explainer invites visitors to look beneath that interface and question what a persona tells us about the system behind it.

The question

Does an AI’s personality tell us what it is capable of — or what it is trying to do?

Explore the shoggoth metaphor, remove its masks, and experiment with the relationship between capabilities and goals.
